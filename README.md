# CS452 Term Paper Report - Monsoon 2025, IITG

## Overview
This repository contains the **term paper report** for the course **CS452: Deep Learning Internals** at the **Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati**, Monsoon 2025 semester.  

The report reviews a recent research paper in the domain of **safety and alignment of large language models (LLMs)**.  

---

## Paper Under Review
- **Target Paper:** [ACL 2024 Long Paper #30](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.30/)  
- **Conference:** *62nd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL 2024)*
- Codebase of the original paper- https://github.com/xyq7/GradSafe

---

##  Repository Contents
This repository includes the structured review of the paper, organized as follows:

1. **Problem the paper addresses**  

2. **Why the problem is important**  

3. **Bottlenecks in existing solutions**  

4. **Intuition of their approach**  
  
5. **Theoretical/experimental results**  
  
6. **Limitations of the approach**  

7. **Understanding of the codebase**  

8. **Suggestions for further improvement**
