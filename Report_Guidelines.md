# CS452 Term Paper Report Guidelines

## Overview
This document provides guidelines for completing your CS452 term paper report reviewing the ACL 2024 research paper.

## Target Paper
- **Source:** [ACL 2024 Long Paper #30](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.30/)
- **Conference:** 62nd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL 2024)

## Report Structure Requirements

### 1. Length and Format
- **Minimum Length:** 8-10 pages (excluding references and appendices)
- **Format Options:** 
  - Markdown format (`CS452_Term_Paper_Report.md`) for online submission
  - LaTeX format (`CS452_Term_Paper_Report.tex`) for professional academic formatting
- **Font:** 12pt, Times New Roman (for LaTeX) or equivalent
- **Line Spacing:** 1.5x
- **Margins:** 1 inch on all sides

### 2. Required Sections

#### Title Page
- Course information (CS452 - Monsoon 2025, IITG)
- Your name and roll number
- Paper title and source
- Submission date

#### Abstract (200-300 words)
- Brief summary of the paper being reviewed
- Your main findings and assessment
- Key conclusions of your review

#### Introduction (1-2 pages)
- Context and background of the research area
- Purpose and scope of your review
- Rationale for paper selection

#### Paper Summary (1-2 pages)
- Clear description of the problem addressed
- Summary of the proposed approach
- Key contributions and claims

#### Methodology Analysis (2-3 pages)
- Detailed analysis of the technical approach
- Assessment of experimental design
- Evaluation of data and resources used

#### Critical Analysis (2-3 pages)
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Theoretical soundness
- Reproducibility assessment
- Comparison with related work

#### Implications and Future Work (1 page)
- Impact on the field
- Practical applications
- Future research directions

#### Conclusion (1 page)
- Summary of your assessment
- Overall evaluation
- Recommendations

### 3. Writing Guidelines

#### Academic Writing Standards
- Use formal, academic language
- Write in third person
- Support all claims with evidence
- Maintain objectivity while providing critical analysis

#### Citation Requirements
- Use proper academic citation format (ACL/IEEE style recommended)
- Include all sources referenced
- Properly cite the main paper being reviewed

#### Critical Analysis Elements
- **Strengths:** What does the paper do well?
- **Weaknesses:** What are the limitations or flaws?
- **Novelty:** How does it differ from existing work?
- **Significance:** What is the impact on the field?
- **Reproducibility:** Can the results be reproduced?
- **Clarity:** Is the paper well-written and clear?

## Evaluation Criteria

Your report will be evaluated based on:

1. **Understanding (25%):** Demonstration of clear understanding of the paper
2. **Critical Analysis (30%):** Quality of critical evaluation and insights
3. **Writing Quality (20%):** Academic writing standards and clarity
4. **Structure and Organization (15%):** Logical flow and proper formatting
5. **References and Citations (10%):** Proper academic referencing

## Tips for Success

### Before Writing
1. Read the paper multiple times
2. Take detailed notes on each section
3. Research the background and related work
4. Identify the key contributions and claims

### During Writing
1. Start with an outline
2. Write the summary sections first
3. Develop your critical analysis with specific examples
4. Support all assessments with evidence from the paper

### After Writing
1. Review for clarity and coherence
2. Check all citations and references
3. Proofread for grammar and spelling
4. Ensure all requirements are met

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Purely Descriptive:** Don't just summarize; provide critical analysis
2. **Lack of Evidence:** Support all claims with specific examples
3. **Unclear Writing:** Ensure your arguments are clear and logical
4. **Missing Context:** Provide sufficient background for your analysis
5. **Poor Organization:** Follow the required structure

## Submission Requirements

- Submit both Markdown and LaTeX versions if possible
- Include a bibliography file if using LaTeX
- Ensure all files are properly named
- Check that all links and references work
