# CS452 Term Paper Report - Evaluation Rubric

Use this rubric to self-assess your report before submission.

## Scoring Scale
- **Excellent (4)**: Exceeds expectations
- **Good (3)**: Meets expectations well
- **Satisfactory (2)**: Meets basic expectations
- **Needs Improvement (1)**: Below expectations

---

## 1. Understanding and Comprehension (25%)

### Paper Understanding
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Demonstrates deep, comprehensive understanding of the paper's content, context, and significance
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Shows solid understanding of main concepts and contributions
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Basic understanding evident, minor gaps in comprehension
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Limited understanding, significant gaps or misunderstandings

### Technical Grasp
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Clearly explains complex technical details and their implications
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Good grasp of technical approach with minor unclear areas
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Basic technical understanding, some confusion evident
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Poor technical understanding, major confusions

**Score: ___/8**

---

## 2. Critical Analysis Quality (30%)

### Depth of Analysis
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Provides insightful, thoughtful analysis that goes beyond surface-level observations
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Good analytical depth with relevant insights
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Basic analysis present, somewhat superficial
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Minimal analysis, mostly descriptive

### Balance and Objectivity
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Balanced evaluation, acknowledges both strengths and weaknesses fairly
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Generally balanced with minor bias
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Somewhat balanced, tends toward one perspective
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Unbalanced, overly positive or negative

### Evidence and Support
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: All claims well-supported with specific examples and evidence
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Most claims supported with adequate evidence
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Some claims supported, others lack evidence
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Few claims supported, mostly unsupported assertions

**Score: ___/12**

---

## 3. Writing Quality and Clarity (20%)

### Academic Writing Style
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Excellent academic writing, appropriate tone and style throughout
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Good academic writing with minor style issues
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Adequate academic writing, some informal language
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Poor academic writing, inappropriate tone

### Clarity and Coherence
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Ideas expressed clearly and logically, easy to follow
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Generally clear with minor unclear passages
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Mostly clear, some confusing sections
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Often unclear, difficult to follow

### Grammar and Mechanics
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Excellent grammar, spelling, and punctuation throughout
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Good language mechanics with few errors
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Adequate mechanics, some errors that don't impede understanding
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Poor mechanics, errors impede understanding

**Score: ___/12**

---

## 4. Structure and Organization (15%)

### Report Structure
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Perfect adherence to required structure, logical flow
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Good structure with minor organizational issues
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Basic structure followed, some organizational problems
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Poor structure, missing required sections

### Transitions and Flow
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Smooth transitions between sections, excellent flow
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Good transitions and flow with minor issues
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Adequate transitions, somewhat choppy flow
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Poor transitions, disjointed presentation

**Score: ___/8**

---

## 5. Citations and References (10%)

### Citation Quality
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: All sources properly cited in consistent academic format
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Most sources properly cited with minor format issues
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Basic citation present, some format inconsistencies
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Poor citation practices, major format issues

### Reference Completeness
- [ ] **Excellent (4)**: Complete bibliography with all necessary information
- [ ] **Good (3)**: Good bibliography with minor omissions
- [ ] **Satisfactory (2)**: Basic bibliography, some missing information
- [ ] **Needs Improvement (1)**: Incomplete or missing bibliography

**Score: ___/8**

---

## Overall Assessment

### Length and Completeness
- [ ] Meets minimum length requirement (8-10 pages)
- [ ] All required sections included
- [ ] Adequate depth in each section

### Specific Requirements Met
- [ ] Target paper correctly identified and reviewed
- [ ] Critical analysis rather than just summary
- [ ] Professional formatting applied
- [ ] Academic integrity maintained

### Self-Assessment Questions
1. Does your report demonstrate genuine engagement with the paper?
2. Would someone reading your report understand both the paper and your evaluation?
3. Have you provided specific examples to support your assessments?
4. Is your analysis balanced and fair?
5. Would you be satisfied with this quality of work?
